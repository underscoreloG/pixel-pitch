# Changelog

The version on the main menu comes from `package.json`. Every release bumps it: minor for new features, patch for fixes.

## 0.9.0 - 2026-10-23

- Headers: aerial duels for high balls (the intended receiver has the edge), headed shots, passes and clearances; hold or tap a kick key as the ball arrives. Chest control for slower balls up to chest height.
- Crosses: the CPU crosses from wide, whipped or floated, and its forwards attack the near post, far post and penalty spot. Keepers come for balls dropping into the six-yard box.
- Offside, with a free kick where the offside player played the ball (Options > OFFSIDE). The CPU holds its runs on the line and won't pass to an offside teammate.
- Two halves with half-time; Options > IF DRAWN: FINAL, EXTRA TIME (then penalties) or PENALTIES. Penalty shootout: five each, then sudden death.
- On CPU penalties you control your keeper: hold up or down as it is struck. Your penalty taker stands ready at the ball.
- Player ratings (pace, shooting, passing, tackling), stamina that drains with sprinting (bar under your player), three substitutions a side from the pause menu; the CPU changes tired players.
- Full-time stats: possession, shots, on target, passing, corners, offsides, fouls, cards.
- Goal kicks go long, to where a teammate wins the race to the ball, instead of a short roll to a defender under pressure.
- CPU defending: tackles whenever in range, a little better; dribbles a step slower than its markers.
- Menus work with a gamepad (d-pad or stick, A, B, Start to pause). Touch screens get an on-screen stick and buttons.

## 0.8.0 - 2026-10-22

- Through balls (W / gamepad Y): played into the space ahead of a teammate, who runs onto it in stride. The CPU plays them too.
- Ball spin: curled shots and crosses bend in the air (barely on the grass).
- Finesse (hold E / RB with shoot or lob): a curled shot that starts outside the post and bends in, or a whipped cross. The CPU curls some shots from the edge of the box.
- Key hints are one compact row of keycaps (keyboard only); the full controls and tips moved to a CONTROLS screen in the main and pause menus.

## 0.7.0 - 2026-10-22

- Faster passes: even a tap arrives at 13-16 m/s, a full bar at about 20 m/s; long passes keep their pace.
- Pass assist prefers the teammate with an open lane, aims from the ball, and allows for your own run; less lead on a moving receiver, so the ball meets him.
- Faster shots: about 84 km/h on a tap, about 126 km/h on a full bar. The power bar fills in 0.8 s instead of 1 s.
- Assisted shots go tighter inside the post, with less error, and volleys stay under the bar.
- Keepers: no more diving early and lying down while a slower shot rolls past; dives vary, so a hard shot into the corner beats them sometimes instead of never.
- CPU shoots a little harder to keep scoring level.

## 0.6.0 - 2026-10-22

- Version number on the main menu.

## 0.5.0 - 2026-10-22

- Firmer assisted passes: the charge sets how firmly the pass arrives; receivers meet the ball's path and take it first time.
- Holding the aim direction after a pass no longer steers the receiver away from the ball.
- Faster, straighter shots; a full bar no longer skies the ball.

## 0.4.0 - 2026-10-22

- Penalties: the keeper commits to a side as the ball is struck, so about 3 in 4 are scored instead of almost none.
- CPU penalty takers pick a random side.

## 0.3.1 - 2026-10-22

- Fix: free kicks, corners and penalties could not be taken if the taker stopped just short of the ball.

## 0.3.0 - 2026-10-22

- Defending: hold S to close down the ball carrier and tackle from a fair angle; getting onto the ball can win it.
- Control switches to the nearest red player when blue wins the ball.
- Assisted passes and shots (Options > KICKS).
